# TextField

Labelled text input. The label sits ABOVE the field (static 13px/700 grey), unlike a floating MUI label. Outlined (boxed) or standard (underline) variants.

```jsx
<TextField label="Email Address" placeholder="Enter your email" helperText="We'll never share it" required />
<TextField label="Notes" multiline rows={4} />
```

Border #8C8C8C → #666 on focus, #CC0000 on error.
